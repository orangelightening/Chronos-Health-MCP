# Emergency Department Visits — Margaret Chen

## 2025-08-18 — First Atrial Fibrillation Episode

**Hospital:** Memorial General Hospital
**Arrival:** 16:45
**Discharge:** 22:30

### Chief Complaint

"I felt my heart start racing suddenly while gardening. It wouldn't slow down. I felt dizzy and had to sit down."

### Assessment

- ECG: Atrial fibrillation with rapid ventricular response, HR 138
- No ST changes, troponin negative (0.01)
- Blood pressure: 148/92 on arrival, 134/84 after 2 hours
- Potassium: 3.5 mmol/L (low-normal)
- No prior history of arrhythmia
- Diagnosis: New-onset paroxysmal atrial fibrillation

### Treatment

- 12-lead ECG and continuous monitoring
- Blood work: CBC, metabolic panel, troponin, thyroid function (TSH normal)
- IV Metoprolol 5mg given — HR reduced to 92
- Spontaneous conversion to sinus rhythm approximately 4 hours after onset
- Cardiology consult — Dr. Okafor (on call)
- TTE (transthoracic echocardiogram) ordered as outpatient

### Discharge Instructions

- Follow up with Dr. Okafor (Cardiology) within 1 week
- Monitor for recurrence — seek emergency care if episode lasts >24 hours
- Avoid excessive caffeine and alcohol
- Reduce stress where possible
- Blood pressure monitoring at home twice daily
- Start Metoprolol 25mg twice daily (prescription provided)
- Start Rivaroxaban 20mg daily (prescription provided) after cardiology confirms

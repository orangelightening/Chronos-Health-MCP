# Surgical Procedures — Margaret Chen

## 2023-04-18 — Cataract Surgery (Left Eye)

**Hospital:** Riverside Eye Surgery Center
**Surgeon:** Dr. Emily Thornton (Ophthalmology)
**Anesthesia:** Local (topical) with mild sedation

### Indication

Age-related cataract (left eye) causing progressive visual impairment. Visual acuity 20/50 left eye, 20/25 right eye. Patient reports difficulty reading and night driving.

### Procedure

Phacoemulsification with intraocular lens implantation (monofocal IOL, distance vision).

### Findings

Moderate nuclear sclerotic cataract. No other ocular pathology noted.

### Complications

None

### Post-Op Instructions

- Prednisolone acetate 1% eye drops: 4x daily for 2 weeks, then taper
- Ofloxacin eye drops: 4x daily for 1 week
- Avoid heavy lifting or bending for 1 week
- Shield at night for 1 week
- No swimming for 2 weeks

### Recovery

Smooth recovery. Visual acuity improved to 20/25 at 4-week follow-up. Right eye cataract not yet requiring surgery.

### Follow-up

- 1 day post-op: Normal — Dr. Thornton
- 1 week post-op: Healing well
- 4 weeks post-op: Final check — discharged to routine eye exams

## 2016-11-03 — Laparoscopic Cholecystectomy

**Hospital:** Memorial General Hospital
**Surgeon:** Dr. Raymond Kwon (General Surgery)
**Anesthesia:** General

### Indication

Symptomatic gallstones. Two episodes of biliary colic in the preceding 3 months. Ultrasound confirmed multiple gallstones, normal bile ducts.

### Procedure

Laparoscopic cholecystectomy (4-port technique). Uneventful procedure.

### Findings

Inflamed gallbladder with multiple cholesterol stones. No common bile duct stones. No signs of cholecystitis.

### Complications

None

### Post-Op Instructions

- Light activity for 2 weeks
- No heavy lifting (>10 lbs) for 4 weeks
- Low-fat diet for 2 weeks, then resume normal diet gradually
- Pain management with acetaminophen — avoid NSAIDs (kidney history)
- Follow-up in 2 weeks

### Recovery

Uneventful recovery. Returned to normal activity within 2 weeks. No digestive issues post-surgery. Small laparoscopic scars well healed.

### Follow-up

- 2 weeks post-op: Incisions healing well, discharged
- No further surgical follow-up needed

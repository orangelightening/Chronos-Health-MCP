# Sample Medical Library — Margaret Chen (Fictional)

This is a **fully populated example library** showing how a real health record collection might look. All data is fictional and does not represent any real person.

## Patient Profile

**Name:** Margaret Chen (fictional)
**Age:** 67
**Chronic Conditions:** Chronic kidney disease (Stage 3a), atrial fibrillation (paroxysmal), hypertension

## Structure

- **lab-results/** — Blood work and metabolic panels across time
  - `2025/` — Baseline kidney labs, routine panels, emergency blood work
  - `2026/` — Follow-up metabolic panel
- **medications/** — Current medications and full change history
  - `current-medications.md` — Active prescriptions with dosages and notes
  - `medication-history.md` — Chronological log of all medication changes
- **doctor-notes/** — Consultations and specialist reports
  - `primary-care-consultations.md` — Family doctor visits
  - `specialist-reports.md` — Nephrology and cardiology consultations
- **hospital-visits/** — Emergency and inpatient records
  - `discharge-summaries.md` — Hospital discharge records
  - `emergency-visits.md` — ER visit reports
- **symptoms/** — Daily tracking and chronic symptom history
  - `daily-symptom-tracking.md` — Day-to-day symptom log
  - `symptom-history.md` — Chronic symptom patterns and trends
- **imaging/** — Radiology reports
  - `ct-scan-reports.md` — CT scan findings
  - `mri-reports.md` — MRI findings
- **procedures/** — Surgical history
  - `surgical-procedures.md` — Operation reports and recovery
- **allergies.md** — Drug, food, and environmental allergies
- **family-history.md** — Family medical history and hereditary conditions
- **personal-health-summary.md` — Top-level health overview
- **vaccination-records.md** — Immunization history

## Things to Try

Once this library is indexed, try asking your AI:

- "What is Margaret's creatinine trend over the past year?"
- "What medications changed after the December AFib episode?"
- "Is there a family history of kidney disease?"
- "What were the findings from the CT scan?"
- "Summarize Margaret's chronic conditions and how they're being managed"
- "What allergens should I be aware of?"
- "How has her blood pressure been tracking?"

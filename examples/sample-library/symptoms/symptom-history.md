## Atrial Fibrillation Episodes

**First Observed:** 2025-08-18
**Frequency:** Episodic (2 confirmed episodes)

### Pattern

Both episodes occurred in the afternoon. First episode (August) while gardening in warm weather. Second episode (December) while resting at home. The December episode was preceded by several days of fatigue and mild muscle cramps (possible early sign of low potassium).

### Severity Over Time

| Date | Duration | HR (peak) | Trigger | Outcome |
|------|----------|-----------|---------|---------|
| 2025-08-18 | ~4 hours | 138 bpm | Exercise, warm weather, possible dehydration | ER visit, spontaneous conversion |
| 2025-12-08 | ~3 hours | 142 bpm | Hypokalemia (K+ = 3.1) | ER visit, IV potassium, spontaneous conversion |

### Relief Measures

- Vagal maneuvers (bearing down) — ineffective during both episodes
- Rest and sitting — moderately helpful for dizziness
- IV Metoprolol (ER) — reduced heart rate

### Treatments

- Rivaroxaban 20mg daily — stroke prevention (started August 2025) — effective, no recurrence since
- Metoprolol 25mg twice daily — rate control (started August 2025) — resting HR now 68-72
- Potassium supplementation — preventing hypokalemia-triggered episodes
- Indapamide reduction — minimizing potassium loss
